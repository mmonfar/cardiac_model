**Verdict key:** consistent = inside the published range; plausible = same order of magnitude, different population or measure;
cannot judge = no comparable published figure was found (**awaiting source**).

## 3. Reading the table

- The model's CICU stays and category rates are inside or near the orders of magnitude that the cited sources
  report for neonatal and infant cardiac surgery; occupancy and waits cannot be judged (no source). That is a face-validity statement, not validation: the sources describe
  other populations (England and Wales, the US, Norway, a small single-centre series) and none reports a waiting-list
  deterioration rate for a managed patient.
- The simulated mean stay (about 6.3 days) is above the nominal 5.2 days of the default mix because each stay is rounded
  up to whole days and because priority operates the long-stay urgent cases first, so they are over-represented among the
  operated patients.
- No source found gives CICU occupancy, a safe occupancy target, or waits for a high-income paediatric cardiac unit.
  Those rows stay "awaiting source" until further sources are reviewed.
- The category escalation placeholders are much higher than any sourced untreated hazard, which means the placeholder set
  probably overstates deterioration for lesions that are not deadlines. The literature-based set is the lower bound of what the
  sources can support; real managed-patient risk sits somewhere between and needs a clinician.
