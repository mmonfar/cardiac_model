# v2.1 versus v2.2 (measured 2026-10-12)

Same service (5 sessions, 10 CICU beds, 60 waiting, 6 referrals a week), 60 seeded years, seeds 5000 to 5059. Do-nothing option.

| Measure | v2.1 (urgency scale, literature rates) | v2.2 (7 diagnoses, published stays) |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting list, now to week 52 | 60 to 110 | 60 to 54 |
| Operations cancelled | 7 | 30 |
| Children who get worse (v2.1: moved up a category; v2.2: none in the defaults, see below) | 34 | not counted |
| Children past their operating window | not measured | 21 |
| Average CICU occupancy | 51% | 54% |

v2.2 recommendation for this service: see the app. Options in v2.2: nothing 54, sessions 0, beds 38, both 0 (list at week 52).

## Why the numbers differ

- v2.2 uses published CICU stays per diagnosis (ToF 4 d, VSD 3 d, ASD 1 d, TGA 7 d, HLHS 19.6 d) in place of the placeholder 14 / 10 / 5 / 3 days.
- The case mix is the share of operations in a national audit (ASD, VSD and ToF dominate), in place of 8 / 15 / 30 / 47%.
- v2.2 drops the five diagnoses with no CICU stay (AVSD, coarctation, PDA, truncus group, Other). Your referrals a week are spread over the seven that remain. This understates total demand.
- v2.2.1: no default deterioration. The one delay odds ratio found (TGA with VSD) states no waiting time and measures complications after surgery, so it is not used. Enter a weekly chance per diagnosis to count it.

## Parity (migrate-with-parity)

`tests/golden/v21.json` holds v2.1.0 outputs (5 configurations x 10 seeds, plus the do-nothing summary). `tests/engine/test_v21_characterisation.py` asserts exact equality. The v2.2 engine change adds a separate delay stream and per-diagnosis counters; v2.1 paths use neither, so all pass unchanged. The two pinned tests (`test_cicu_pinned.py`, `test_ward_referral_pinned.py`) are unchanged.
